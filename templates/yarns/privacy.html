Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your username and, if you sign in with Google, your email address. We also store the yarns you mark as favourites.

We do not collect payment information, sell your data, or use it for advertising.

Uploaded images

If you upload an image to find a colour match, it is processed on our server to extract the dominant colour and then immediately discarded. Uploaded images are never stored or shared.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. If you use Google Sign-In, Google may set its own cookies subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Google OAuth — optional sign-in via Google. We receive your name and email address from Google if you choose this option.
  • Ko-fi — a voluntary tip widget. Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies if you interact with it.

Product images and data

Yarn names, images, and product information displayed on this site belong to their respective brands. They are shown for colour reference purposes only, and each result links back to the original product page.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Under GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, or export your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Find me on Github.
